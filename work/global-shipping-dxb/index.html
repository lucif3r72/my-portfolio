GLOBAL SHIPPING

Logistics & Freight Company Website

The first-ever website for Global Shipping DXB, a logistics company with no prior digital presence, built to establish trust and clearly present their services through a conversion-focused funnel that drives visitors toward getting in touch.

Overview Research Process Design Results Reflection
01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

Global Shipping DXB, a logistics company, had no digital presence before this project — no website, no online way for potential clients to discover or trust them. The core challenge was building that trust from zero while clearly presenting a logistics business's services to visitors who may know nothing about the company going in.

Logistics is a trust-driven industry: clients are handing over freight, timelines, and operations to a company they need to believe can deliver, so the site had to do more than just look presentable.

My Contribution
UI/UX Design Frontend Development Competitive Research Conversion Funnel Design
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

I researched how competing logistics and freight websites present themselves, then designed Global Shipping DXB's site to improve on that baseline rather than follow it directly.

The whole site is structured as a funnel: every section is built to move a visitor from "just looking" toward reaching out, so services, credibility signals, and the contact path all work together instead of sitting as disconnected pages.

Key Research Findings
01

Starting From Zero

Global Shipping DXB had no website or online presence at all, meaning every visitor's first impression of the company had to be built from this one site.

02

Trust Is the Product, Too

In logistics, clients are trusting a company with real freight and timelines — the site needed to signal reliability, not just describe services.

03

Competitors Set the Bar Low

Existing logistics websites in the space were often generic or hard to navigate, leaving real room to stand out with a clearer, more considered experience.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
Trust

Credibility Built Into Every Section

Rather than a single "About" page, trust and credibility signals are woven throughout the site so a visitor doesn't have to hunt for reasons to believe in the company.

Conversion

A Funnel, Not a Brochure

Every section moves a visitor a step closer to making contact, instead of just listing services and leaving the next step up to them.

Services

Clear Service Presentation

Logistics offerings are presented clearly and directly, so a first-time visitor with no industry background can understand what the company actually does.

Positioning

Built to Outperform the Category

Designed against a direct read of competing logistics websites, aiming to be clearer and more considered than the category norm.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

Global Shipping DXB just launched, so there's no performance data to report yet. What the site does give the company, for the first time, is a real digital foundation — every future inquiry and every piece of feedback will be the first the business has ever had online.

First-Ever Digital presence for Global Shipping DXB
Live Launched at globalshippingdxb.com
Foundation Ready for growth, inquiries, and feedback going forward
04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

Building a logistics company's first website from zero reinforced how much weight "trust" carries in an industry where the product itself is invisible until something goes right, or wrong.

Designing against a direct read of competitors, rather than working in a vacuum, made every decision sharper — I could see exactly where the category fell short and design specifically against those gaps.
