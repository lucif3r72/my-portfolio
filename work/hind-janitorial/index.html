HIND

Janitorial & Maintenance Service Website

The first-ever website for Hind Janitorial & Maintenance, a Canada-based service company, built to establish trust and make booking their janitorial and maintenance services effortless for both new and returning customers.

01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

Hind Janitorial & Maintenance, a Canada-based service company, had no website before this project — no way for potential customers to discover them, understand their services, or book anything online. The core challenge was building trust for a hands-on service business while making it effortless for visitors to actually request a booking.

Service businesses like this compete on reliability as much as price — a visitor deciding whether to trust a company with access to their space needs to feel confident before they'll ever reach out.

My Contribution
UI/UX Design Frontend Development Competitive Research Booking Flow Design
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

I designed and built the entire website, grounding key decisions in research into competitor sites within the janitorial and maintenance market rather than designing in a vacuum.

The site centers on a straightforward path to booking: a contact form built specifically for requesting services, so visitors can move from browsing services to reaching out without friction.

Key Research Findings
01

No Digital Presence to Build From

Hind Janitorial had no prior website, meaning every visitor's first impression had to be established from this one site.

02

Trust Precedes Booking

For a service business entering someone's space, visitors need to feel confident in the company before they'll commit to a booking request.

03

Competitors Set a Baseline

Researching other janitorial and maintenance websites revealed a fairly generic category, leaving room to present services more clearly than the norm.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
Trust

Credibility-First Presentation

Every section was designed to build confidence before asking for a booking, since visitors are trusting the company with access to their space.

Booking

Straightforward Service Requests

A dedicated contact form lets visitors request services directly, without needing to call or email to get a quote started.

Positioning

Grounded in Market Research

Key design decisions were shaped by studying competitor websites in the janitorial and maintenance space, aiming to present services more clearly than the category norm.

Access

Built for Every Visitor

Designed to work equally well for service seekers researching options and general visitors just exploring, so the site never feels narrowly built for one audience.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

The site is still relatively new, so there's no hard performance data yet, but traffic has been growing steadily since launch — a promising early signal for a business that had no digital presence at all before this.

04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

Building Hind Janitorial's first website reinforced how much a service business's website has to do the work of a first handshake — visitors are deciding whether to trust a company before they've spoken to anyone.

Grounding decisions in competitor research, even for a straightforward service site, made the difference between a generic template feel and a site that actually presents the business clearly.
