MANNINGFLO

Seafarer Manning Applicant Tracking System

An in-house applicant tracking system built at Werq Labs to replace a long, manual seafarer hiring process — forms and HR interviews — with a structured, trackable manning workflow for the shipping industry.

UI/UX Design Design System Frontend Development ATS / Recruitment Tech
CLIENT Werq Labs (Internal Product)
ROLE UI/UX Designer & Frontend Developer
PLATFORM Web Application
STATUS In Production (Internal)
YEAR 2026
Overview Research Process Design Results Reflection
01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

Seafarer hiring is a long process — forms, HR interviews, and manual coordination between candidates and recruiters — with no dedicated system holding the whole pipeline together. Werq Labs set out to build ManningFlo internally to cut that time down and make life easier for the shipping industry's hiring teams.

Without a structured system, the process was also prone to data integrity issues: candidate records living across scattered forms and notes rather than a single, reliable source of truth.

My Contribution
UI/UX Design Design System Frontend Development Applicant Tracking Flow Design
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

I designed and built ManningFlo end-to-end, from the design system through to the product UI, as an applicant tracking system purpose-built for seafarer manning rather than a generic ATS retrofitted for maritime hiring.

Because engineering used AI-assisted tooling to build the production app, I also built a working HTML/CSS/JS reference implementation of the UI, giving developers an exact, functioning reference to build from rather than static mockups alone.

Key Research Findings
01

No Single Source of Truth

Candidate information lived across forms and manual notes rather than one trackable record, creating data integrity problems throughout the pipeline.

02

A Long, Manual Pipeline

Hiring moved through forms and HR interviews with no system tracking where a candidate actually stood in the process at any given point.

03

Hiring at Industry Scale

The shipping industry's hiring volume meant the fix had to hold up for recruiters managing many candidates at once, not just a handful.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
Candidate Records

Structured Candidate Profiles

Every candidate gets a detailed profile — experience, history, and status — replacing scattered documents with one reliable record recruiters can trust.

Outreach

Built-In Candidate Communication

Recruiters can reach out to candidates directly from within the system, keeping outreach tied to the candidate's record instead of living in a separate inbox.

Handoff

A Working Reference, Not Just Mockups

I built the frontend in HTML/CSS/JS as a functioning reference implementation, giving engineering an exact, working UI to build the production app from.

Design System

Built From Zero, Built to Scale

Every component came from a design system built specifically for ManningFlo, giving the product room to grow new features without losing consistency.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

ManningFlo is now in production internally, and it has meaningfully cut down the time HR teams spend on manual seafarer manning work, while a structured application flow has eliminated the data integrity problems the old process was prone to.

Significant Time saved on manual manning work
Eliminated Data integrity issues via structured flow
Live In production, used internally at Werq Labs
04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

Building ManningFlo end-to-end, from design system through to a working frontend reference, showed me how much smoother design-to-development handoff can be when designers hand over something that actually runs, not just a static file.

Solving for an industry as specific as maritime hiring reinforced that the best systems come from understanding the actual manual process first — the structure of ManningFlo followed directly from how HR teams were already trying, and struggling, to track candidates.
