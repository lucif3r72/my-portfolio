KAZROOR

Logistics Company Website

The first-ever website for Kazroor, a Dubai-based logistics company, built from zero digital presence into a credible, inquiry-driven site shaped by research into how other logistics brands present themselves online.

CLIENT Kazroor

YEAR 2026
Overview Research Process Design Results Reflection
01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

Kazroor, a Dubai-based logistics company, had no website or online presence before this project. Like most businesses in the freight and logistics space, the company needed a way for potential clients to actually find and evaluate them before ever picking up the phone.

The core challenge was familiar: build credibility from nothing, in an industry where clients are trusting a company with real cargo, timelines, and operations, not just browsing a catalog.

My Contribution
UI/UX Design Frontend Development Competitive Research Conversion Funnel Design
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

As with other logistics projects, I started by looking at how competing freight and shipping companies present themselves online, then used that research to shape a site that reads as more credible and easier to navigate than the category standard.

The site is structured to move a visitor from first impression to inquiry with as little friction as possible — services, credibility signals, and a clear path to contact all reinforcing each other rather than sitting on separate, disconnected pages.

Key Research Findings
01

No Prior Presence

Kazroor had no digital footprint at all, meaning the site had to establish the company's identity from the very first visit.

02

Trust Comes Before the Quote

Logistics clients are committing cargo and deadlines to a company, so credibility had to be established before a service inquiry felt reasonable.

03

A Familiar, Generic Category

Competing logistics sites tended to follow the same conventions, leaving room for Kazroor to feel more considered and direct.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
Trust

Credibility Woven Throughout

Rather than isolating trust signals to one section, they're distributed across the site so a visitor is building confidence at every step, not just on an About page.

Conversion

One Continuous Path to Inquiry

Every section nudges a visitor toward reaching out, keeping the funnel from feeling like a set of disconnected pages.

Services

Direct, No-Jargon Service Descriptions

Logistics offerings are explained plainly, so a visitor unfamiliar with the industry still understands exactly what Kazroor does.

Positioning

Designed Against the Category Norm

Built with a direct read of competing logistics sites in mind, aiming to feel more deliberate than the generic industry template.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

Kazroor's website is newly launched, so there's no performance data to share yet. What it does give the company is a real, first-ever digital front door to build on.

First-Ever Digital presence for Kazroor
Live Launched at kazroor.com
Foundation Ready for inquiries and growth going forward
04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

Working on a second logistics website so soon after Global Shipping DXB reinforced that "trust-first" isn't a one-off decision, it's a pattern this entire industry needs, and getting faster at applying it well is its own kind of progress.

Every company in this space starts from the same blank page, but the details, how clearly services are explained, how naturally the funnel flows, are what actually separate one from another.
