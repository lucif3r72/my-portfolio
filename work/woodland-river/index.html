WORK / WOODLAND RIVER

WOODLAND

Villa Showcase Website

The first-ever website for Woodland River, showcasing a range of villas in Alibaug, Maharashtra, built to present each property visually, build trust for a high-value booking decision, and turn interest into inquiries.

CLIENT Woodland River
ROLE UI/UX Designer & Frontend Developer
PLATFORM Website
STATUS In Production
YEAR 2026
Overview Research Process Design Results Reflection
01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

Woodland River had no website before this project — no way to showcase its range of villas in Alibaug or let interested visitors explore properties before reaching out. For a high-value purchase or rental decision like this, visuals and trust matter as much as information.

The core challenge was making each villa feel desirable and credible from the very first screen, while giving visitors a clear, low-friction way to move from browsing to inquiry.

My Contribution
UI/UX Design Frontend Development Visual Storytelling Booking Flow Design
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

I designed and built every page of the site around presenting the villas the way they deserve to be seen: strong galleries, individual listing pages for each property, and clear amenities so visitors know exactly what they're considering.

Location context for Alibaug was woven throughout, giving visitors a sense of place alongside the properties themselves, and every villa page leads naturally into an inquiry flow rather than leaving visitors to hunt for a way to get in touch.

Key Research Findings
01

No Way to Explore Remotely

With no website, interested buyers or renters had no way to browse Woodland River's villas before making direct contact.

02

High-Value Decisions Need Trust

A villa purchase or rental is a significant commitment, so visuals and presentation had to build confidence, not just show photos.

03

Location Is Part of the Pitch

Alibaug itself is part of what makes these villas appealing, so the site needed to sell the place as much as the properties.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

Since launch, Woodland River's website has received strong positive feedback, giving the business its first real way to showcase villas to interested buyers and renters online.

04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

Designing Woodland River reinforced how much presentation carries weight in a high-value decision — the site had to do the work a good sales conversation does, just visually and asynchronously.

Weaving Alibaug's sense of place into the site alongside the villas themselves was a reminder that context sells almost as much as the product itself, especially for a purchase this considered.
