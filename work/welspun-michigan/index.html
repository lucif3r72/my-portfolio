WORK / WELSPUN MICHIGAN

WELSPUN

Immersive Infrastructure Experience

A modern digital experience for Welspun Michigan, a vertical of Welspun World, centered on an interactive 3D city that visually demonstrates how its infrastructure solutions operate across an urban ecosystem.

UI Design 3D Experience Design Web Design Enterprise Storytelling
CLIENT Welspun World (Welspun Michigan)
ROLE UI/UX Designer
PLATFORM Website
STATUS In Production
YEAR 2024
Overview Research Process Design Results Reflection
01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

Infrastructure and industrial operations are complex and difficult to communicate through traditional websites. Stakeholders often struggle to understand how systems function at a city scale.

The challenge was to translate technical processes into an experience that's intuitive, visually compelling, and accessible to decision-makers, partners, and the general audience alike.

My Contribution
UI Design 3D Experience Design Storytelling & Animation Direction Brand Consistency
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

The primary goal was a digital platform that clearly explains Welspun Michigan's operations while reinforcing Welspun World's scale and innovation leadership, using immersive 3D visuals to make complex concepts easy to grasp without technical jargon.

A custom-built 3D city environment animates how the infrastructure solutions operate in real-world scenarios, with users exploring different parts of the city through guided animations and storytelling.

Key Research Findings
01

Complexity Doesn't Translate to Text

Infrastructure and industrial operations proved genuinely difficult to communicate through traditional, text-heavy corporate websites.

02

Scale Is Hard to Picture

Stakeholders struggled to understand how systems function at city scale when presented as static pages rather than a spatial, explorable story.

03

A Brand Signal, Not Just Info

The platform needed to explain operations while also reinforcing Welspun World's scale and innovation leadership to partners and decision-makers.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
Core Experience

Custom 3D City Environment

A custom-built 3D city animates how Welspun Michigan's infrastructure solutions operate in real-world scenarios, replacing static diagrams with an explorable environment.

Narrative

Guided Animations & Storytelling

Users explore different parts of the city and understand system flow through guided animations, turning a technical explanation into a story that unfolds spatially.

Interface

Clean, Minimal UI

The UI stays clean and minimal to keep focus on the 3D experience, while maintaining brand consistency across every screen.

Engineering

Performance-Conscious 3D

The immersive 3D experience was built with performance in mind, keeping the site usable across devices instead of trading usability for spectacle.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

The 3D-driven experience significantly improved engagement and understanding of Welspun Michigan's operations. Stakeholders were able to visualize infrastructure impact at a city level, leading to clearer communication during presentations.

Higher Stakeholder engagement & understanding
Clearer Communication in presentations & discussions
Live In production
04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

This project highlighted how immersive design can transform enterprise storytelling. By combining UX principles with 3D visualization, the platform bridges the gap between technical complexity and human understanding.

It strengthened my experience designing high-impact digital experiences for large-scale organizations, where the story has to work for engineers and executives alike.
