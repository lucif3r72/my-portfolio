STELLAR AMC

Wealth Advisory Website Redesign

A full redesign of Stellar AMC's website, a Dubai-based financial advisory firm, rebuilding a trust-first narrative around long-term wealth planning after the previous site failed to represent what the company actually stands for.

ROLE UI/UX Designer & Frontend Developer
PLATFORM Website

YEAR 2026
Overview Research Process Design Results Reflection
01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

Stellar AMC, a Dubai-based financial advisory firm, already had a website, but it didn't represent what the company actually stands for: sustainable, personalized, long-term financial guidance built on trust and confidentiality. This redesign wasn't a cosmetic refresh, it was a rebuild of the narrative itself.

The core challenge was industry research: understanding how wealth advisory actually works, identifying the site's primary user, and shaping an overall story and funnel that would make sense for someone making a long-term, high-trust financial decision.

My Contribution
UI/UX Design Frontend Development Industry Research Narrative & Funnel Design
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

Before touching any screens, I researched the wealth advisory industry to understand how firms like Stellar AMC actually build trust with clients making long-term, high-stakes decisions, and who the site's primary visitor really is.

That research shaped the site's story: philosophy and trust established first, services presented once credibility is built, and a clear path to requesting a consultation once a visitor is ready, rather than asking for that commitment upfront.

Key Research Findings
01

The Old Site Misrepresented the Brand

Stellar AMC's previous website existed, but didn't communicate the personalized, trust-first approach the company actually takes with clients.

02

Trust Has to Be Earned Before the Ask

Wealth advisory clients are making long-term, high-trust decisions, meaning credibility had to be established before any service or consultation request.

03

A Long-Term, Personal Relationship

Stellar AMC's own positioning centers on relationships that extend across generations, which the site needed to reflect rather than read as a generic advisory pitch.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
Narrative

Trust and Philosophy First

The site opens with Stellar AMC's approach and values before ever presenting services, establishing credibility before making any ask.

Positioning

Built for a Long-Term Relationship

The narrative reflects Stellar AMC's actual positioning around multi-generational, personal financial relationships, not a generic advisory pitch.

Funnel

A Consultation Ask, Earned Not Assumed

The path to requesting a consultation comes after trust is established, rather than being pushed on a visitor immediately.

Research

Grounded in the Industry, Not Guesswork

Every structural decision followed from understanding how wealth advisory actually works and who the primary visitor is, rather than assumptions.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

Stellar AMC's site is fairly new, so there's no performance data to report yet, but it now reflects, for the first time, what the company actually stands for.

Rebuilt Brand narrative now matches Stellar AMC's positioning
Live In production
Foundation Ready for consultation requests and growth going forward
04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

Stellar AMC was a reminder that a redesign isn't always about a site looking outdated, sometimes the problem is that it simply doesn't say what the company actually is.

Researching an industry as trust-dependent as wealth advisory before touching any screens shaped the entire funnel — understanding the primary user's actual decision-making process mattered more than any individual page design choice.
