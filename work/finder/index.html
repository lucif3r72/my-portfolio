FINDER

Smart Restaurant Discovery & Offers

A conceptual restaurant discovery platform for the UAE market, combining discovery, real-time offers, and loyalty rewards into one experience — with QR-based discount redemption users and restaurants can both trust.

UX Design UI Design Interaction Design Rewards System
CLIENT Concept Project
ROLE UI/UX Designer
PLATFORM Mobile App (iOS & Android)
STATUS Conceptual (End-to-End Designed)
YEAR 2025
Overview Research Process Design Results Reflection
01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

The food discovery space is crowded with platforms that either focus on listings or discounts, but rarely both in a meaningful way. Users often struggle to compare prices, understand offers, and trust whether discounts will actually be honored at checkout.

Designing a system that balances discovery, transparency, rewards, and in-person redemption without friction was the core challenge.

My Contribution
End-to-End UX UI Design QR Redemption Flow Rewards System Design
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

Finder needed to clearly surface offers, pricing for two people, ratings, and cuisine types while introducing a reliable QR-based discount redemption system — with a long-term loyalty loop built on coins users can redeem for rewards.

A clean, card-based UI highlights restaurants, categories, and trending offers. Restaurant detail pages focus on decision-making essentials, and discounts are applied through an in-app QR code so both users and restaurants can trust the transaction.

Key Research Findings
01

Discovery or Discounts, Never Both

Existing platforms typically optimize for listings or for discounts, rarely both, leaving users to juggle multiple apps for one dining decision.

02

Trust Breaks at Checkout

Users often couldn't tell whether an advertised discount would actually be honored in person, undermining confidence right at the moment of redemption.

03

Missing Decision-Making Info

Price range for two, ratings, and cuisine type were frequently missing or buried, making it hard to compare options at a glance.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
Discovery

Card-Based, Offer-Driven

A clean, card-based UI surfaces restaurants, categories, and trending offers together, replacing the list-heavy, generic browsing of existing platforms.

Redemption

QR-Based Checkout

Discounts are applied through a QR code generated within the app, giving both users and restaurants a verifiable, trustworthy redemption moment.

Decision Info

Essentials First

Restaurant detail pages foreground price range for two, ratings, offers, and cuisine type — the exact information users need to decide, and nothing else.

Loyalty

Coins & Rewards Loop

A built-in rewards system converts savings and spend into coins, redeemable for future offers, encouraging repeat visits instead of one-off use.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

User testing and validation sessions showed high confidence in the QR-based redemption flow and strong engagement with the rewards system. Test users reported clearer decision-making and increased willingness to try new restaurants.

High Confidence in QR redemption flow
Strong Engagement with rewards system
Scalable Foundation for partnerships & expansion
04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

Finder explores how discovery, savings, and loyalty can coexist in a single experience. This project let me design not just screens, but a complete value loop between users and restaurants.

It reinforced the importance of trust, transparency, and simplicity, especially in everyday consumer products where a single confusing moment can lose the user.
