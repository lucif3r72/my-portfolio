AI SCRIBE

Intelligent Clinical Documentation Assistant

A conceptual healthcare product that documents consultations, prescriptions, and clinical notes in real time, eliminating the need for human scribes so doctors can focus on patients instead of screens.

UX Design UI Design AI Product Design Workflow Design
CLIENT Concept Project
ROLE UI/UX Designer
PLATFORM Web & Tablet (Clinic & Hospital Use)
STATUS Conceptual (End-to-End Designed)
YEAR 2025
Overview Research Process Design Results Reflection
01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

Doctors spend a significant portion of their time documenting consultations rather than interacting with patients. Manual note-taking and human scribes introduce delays, errors, and high operational costs.

The challenge was to design an AI-powered system doctors could trust: accurate, unobtrusive, compliant, and easy to use during live appointments across clinics of all sizes.

My Contribution
End-to-End UX UI Design Clinical Workflow Design Concept Validation
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

The goal was to reduce documentation burden while improving clinical accuracy. AI Scribe needed to seamlessly capture conversations, generate structured medical records, and assist with prescriptions and follow-ups, without disrupting the patient conversation.

A calm, minimal interface was designed for high-pressure medical environments. Live transcription runs quietly in the background while structured notes are auto-generated in real time, and doctors review, edit, and approve outputs with minimal interaction.

Key Research Findings
01

Documentation Steals Patient Time

A significant share of consultation time goes to documentation rather than the patient in front of the doctor, splitting attention at the worst moment.

02

Costly, Error-Prone Process

Manual note-taking and human scribes introduce delays, errors, and high operational costs that scale poorly as clinics grow.

03

A High Trust Bar

Any AI system entering the exam room had to be accurate, unobtrusive, and compliant before doctors would trust it during live appointments.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
Capture

Quiet Live Transcription

Transcription runs quietly in the background during the appointment, replacing manual note-taking or a human scribe with a system that stays out of the conversation.

Output

Structured Notes, Auto-Generated

Structured medical records are generated in real time from the conversation, replacing error-prone manual documentation with a consistent, structured format.

Control

Review & Approve, Not Replace

Doctors review, edit, and approve every AI-generated output with minimal interaction — the system assists judgment, it doesn't replace it.

Scale

Clinic to Hospital Ready

The same workflow supports appointment context, prescriptions, and follow-up summaries across single practices and large hospital systems alike.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

Concept validation sessions with healthcare professionals indicated strong interest in reducing documentation time and improving focus during patient visits. Simulated workflows showed potential time savings per consultation.

~40% Simulated time saved per consultation
Strong Clinician interest in validation sessions
Scalable Foundation for AI-assisted clinical ops
04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

Designing AI Scribe emphasized the importance of trust in healthcare UX. The goal was not to replace doctors' judgment, but to remove repetitive administrative work.

This project strengthened my approach to designing AI systems that feel supportive, transparent, and human-centered in critical environments.
