EN-BLOCS

Collective-Sale Intelligence Platform

01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

In Singapore's collective-sale (en bloc) market, an opportunity depends on how many owners in a development are willing to sell. En-Blocs is built around that signal: it lets licensed agents discover developments, track owner intent, and see when a development is ready to pursue, all in one place.

The challenge was a product with three audiences and strict gates. Agents must be verified and subscribed before they see opportunities, owners record their intent and property details, and admins verify agents, manage developments and keep government data in sync. Each role needed its own tools, but all three had to feel like one product.

02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

I designed the product as three connected portals on one interface language. Agents move from sign-up and OTP verification through a profile and licence check to a dashboard that leads with what matters: new opportunities, rising interest and developments that have reached the threshold. Unsubscribed areas show a locked preview, so access rules are visible instead of hidden.

Admins get the other side of those rules: a verification queue with an in-app document viewer, subscription management, government data sync and API configuration, an audit log and user management. Owners get a property dashboard, notifications and a community forum with report-and-review moderation, plus mobile screens. Everything was designed to the client's request, built on an understanding of the whole en-bloc process and how each part of it functions.

Key Requirements
01

Trust Before Access

Agents had to prove who they were, with licence numbers, agency details and supporting documents, before seeing opportunities, and subscriptions decided which areas were open to them.

02

Signal Over Noise

Agents needed to see at a glance where owner interest was growing and which developments were ready to pursue, not wade through raw tables.

03

Three Roles, One Product

Agents, owners and admins needed different views, tools and permissions, without the product feeling like three separate apps.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
Onboarding

Verification Built Into Sign-Up

Sign-up, OTP and a profile and verification step collect licence and agency details and supporting documents, with a completion indicator so agents always know what is left to do.

Dashboard

A Dashboard That Leads With Opportunity

Summary cards for total available, new since last login, increased interest, reached threshold, strong “OK to sell” and upcoming sell-by dates put the next action first, with a filtered view for developments that have reached the threshold.

Data Display

Owner Intent at a Glance

Stacked bars show OK to sell, Never and Unknown for each development, backed by an owners table with intent, wish price and ownership type, and reports that compare wish prices with market data.

Access & Admin

Access Rules Designed In

Unsubscribed areas show a locked preview, and admins manage the rules behind it through a verification queue with an in-app document viewer and reject reasons, subscription management and an audit log.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

En-Blocs is designed end to end across three role-based portals, documented in a Figma file organised by portal. The screens were designed to the client's request, grounded in the full en-bloc process. There are no usage metrics to report for this design work.

04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

Designing for three audiences made access and status the main design material: verified or unverified, subscribed or locked, OK to sell or never. Giving those states a consistent visual language, with chips, bars and locked previews, kept dense data screens scannable.

Designing to the client's request meant understanding the whole en-bloc process first: how each step works, and who needs what at each stage. Organising the work by portal then kept every role consistent.
