RENTTRO

Unified Rental Platform

A UAE-based rental platform for tourists and residents to rent cars, yachts, and water sports equipment from verified partners, simplifying complex rental rules into a secure, seamless booking experience.

UX Design UI Design Design System Role-Based Dashboards
CLIENT Renttro
ROLE UI/UX Designer
PLATFORM Web & Mobile (iOS & Android)
STATUS Pilot Launch (UAE)
YEAR 2025
Overview Research Process Design Results Reflection
01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

Renttro spans multiple rental categories — cars, yachts, and water sports — each governed by different pricing models, security deposits, license requirements, age limits, time slots, and cancellation policies. These rules often overwhelm users, especially tourists.

At the same time, rental partners were working with fragmented tools and unclear inventory management. The challenge was to simplify complexity without hiding critical information.

My Contribution
End-to-End UX Design System Booking Flow Design Role-Based Dashboards
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

I designed a clean, intuitive experience that makes renting feel effortless while maintaining transparency and trust, built to serve tourists, residents, rental partners, and admins equally well through a single, unified design system.

A modular UI adapts across rental categories while staying consistent. Complex rental rules are broken into guided steps with smart defaults, and role-based dashboards keep partners and admins operationally clear.

Key Research Findings
01

Rule Overload

Different pricing models, deposits, license requirements, age limits, and cancellation policies across categories left tourists overwhelmed before they could even book.

02

Fragmented Partner Tools

Rental partners managed inventory and availability across disconnected tools, with no clear operational picture of their own listings.

03

Four Very Different Users

Tourists, residents, rental partners, and admins all needed the platform to work equally well, ruling out a single generic experience for everyone.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
Booking Flow

Contextual, Guided Steps

Complex rental rules are presented contextually and broken into guided steps with smart defaults, replacing the long, manual flows typical of traditional rental platforms.

Trust

Verified Partners, Transparent Pricing

Trust indicators — verified partners, secure payments, and transparent pricing breakdowns — are surfaced early, replacing the hidden add-ons common elsewhere.

Operations

Role-Based Dashboards

Separate, purpose-built dashboards give users, partners, and admins exactly the tools they need, replacing fragmented spreadsheets and disconnected systems.

System

Modular Category UI

One design system adapts across cars, yachts, and water sports while staying visually consistent, giving Renttro a scalable base for adding new rental categories.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

During the pilot phase, Renttro reduced booking drop-offs and let users complete rentals significantly faster than traditional flows. Rental partners reported improved clarity in managing listings and availability.

38% Reduction in booking drop-offs
2.8× Faster rental completion
UAE Pilot Scalable foundation for category expansion
04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

Designing Renttro reinforced the importance of explaining complexity rather than hiding it. High-value rentals depend on trust, clarity, and confidence.

By breaking rules into human-friendly moments, Renttro transforms a traditionally stressful process into a seamless, reliable experience for tourists, residents, partners, and admins alike.
