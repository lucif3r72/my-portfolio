AGEES

Educational & Career Consulting Website

The first-ever website for AGEES, a Canada-based educational and career consulting company, designed and built from zero digital presence into a trustworthy, booking-driven site for a largely non-technical, first-time audience.

01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

AGEES had no website before this project — no digital identity, no online trust signals for people researching career and educational consultants. Before designing anything, I studied the career-consulting market to understand how similar consultants present themselves, so AGEES could be represented properly within it.

The core challenge was building a digital identity from zero for a client whose own audience often isn't deeply technical. Many visitors researching a consultant are doing so for the first time, and anything that felt complicated risked losing them before they even reached the services page.

My Contribution
UI/UX Design Frontend Development Market Research Booking Integration
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

The site needed to feel trustworthy and modern without ever feeling complicated, and it needed a clear path from "just looking" to "booked a consultation." I kept the structure deliberately focused: four pages — Home, About, Services, and Contact — built as a funnel rather than a scattering of information.

Each page moves a visitor a step closer to applying for a consultation, and an appointment-booking calendar is integrated directly into the site, so scheduling never requires a separate email or phone call.

Key Research Findings
01

Starting From Zero

AGEES had no prior website or online trust signals at all, competing against consultants who already had established web presences and reviews.

02

First-Time, Non-Technical Visitors

Many people researching career or educational consulting were doing so for the first time and needed a site that never felt intimidating or complex.

03

Two Services, One Identity

Educational consulting and career consulting needed to read as one coherent brand, rather than two separate, competing offers on the same site.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
Information Architecture

Four Focused Pages

Deliberately kept to Home, About, Services, and Contact rather than a sprawling site, so a first-time visitor is never more than a click away from the page they need.

Conversion

A Funnel, Not a Brochure

Every page nudges the visitor a step closer to applying for a consultation, instead of just describing services in isolation and hoping they find their own way to contact.

Booking

Integrated Appointment Calendar

An appointment-booking calendar lives directly on the site, replacing the friction of emailing or calling to schedule a consultation.

Visual System

Minimal, Modern, Non-Intimidating

A clean, uncluttered visual language built specifically for visitors who may be researching a consultant online for the very first time.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

Since launch, AGEES has seen a real increase in consultation activity on what is the brand's first-ever website — every visitor and every booking is entirely incremental to a business that previously had no online presence at all.

40% Increase in consultation bookings
~1K Monthly website visitors
Positive User feedback since launch
04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

Building AGEES's first website reinforced how much market research matters before any screen gets designed. Understanding how other consultants present themselves online shaped nearly every decision that followed, from page structure to tone.

Designing for a genuinely non-technical, first-time audience is a different discipline than designing for people already comfortable online — every choice had to earn its place instead of assuming familiarity.
