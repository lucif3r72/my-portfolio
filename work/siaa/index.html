SIAA

AI-Powered ATS & Candidate Portal

An AI-integrated applicant tracking system used by companies from MSMEs to huge corporates, with resume parsing, screening, search, and recommendation AI built into every stage of hiring, alongside a candidate-facing portal on the same platform.

01 · OVERVIEW

The Problem

Hiring, before SIAA, was a manual process at almost every stage: creating a job listing, shortlisting applicants, and screening candidates to judge whether they were actually a good fit all relied on HR teams doing the work by hand.

The platform also needed to work for a huge range of company sizes, from MSMEs hiring a handful of roles to large corporates running high-volume recruitment, without becoming either too simple for one end or too complex for the other.

My Contribution
UI/UX Design Design System AI Product Design Candidate Portal Design
02 · APPROACH

How We Solved It

I designed every part of the product — job creation, candidate management, interviews, and the surrounding workflows — with AI woven into the hiring lifecycle rather than bolted on as a separate feature. AI resume parsing, AI screening, AI search, and AI recommendations sit inside the existing HR workflow instead of asking recruiters to learn a new one.

Rather than building separate experiences for small and large companies, I designed one consistent system that scales with the size of the hiring team using it — the same interface works whether five roles are open or five hundred.

Key Research Findings
01

Manual at Every Stage

Job creation, shortlisting, and screening were all manual, meaning HR teams spent time on repetitive work instead of actual hiring decisions.

02

Screening Was Guesswork at Scale

Judging whether a candidate was a good fit relied entirely on manual review, which broke down fast once application volume grew.

03

One Platform, Very Different Teams

MSMEs and large corporates needed to use the same system, despite having very different hiring volume and structure.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS
AI

AI Woven Into the Workflow

Resume parsing, screening, search, and recommendations happen inside the existing hiring flow, so AI assists the decision instead of creating a separate tool to check.

Scale

One Design, Every Company Size

A single consistent interface scales from MSMEs hiring a few roles to large corporates running high-volume recruitment, without a separate "enterprise" mode.

Candidates

A Dedicated Candidate Portal

Candidates get their own portal into the same platform, keeping the application and status-tracking experience as considered as the recruiter side.

Workflow

Designed for the Full Hiring Lifecycle

Jobs, candidates, and interviews were designed as one connected system, not as separate modules bolted together.

03 · RESULTS

Outcome

SIAA is live in production, handling real hiring volume with AI-assisted screening at the core. Adoption started slow — the AI-driven workflow was new to HR teams at first — but it has since become a natural part of how they hire.

~30% Reduction in hiring time
~90% AI screening accuracy
10K+ Candidate records handled
04 · REFLECTION

What I Learned

SIAA reinforced that AI in a product like this works best when it's invisible until it's useful — recruiters don't want to "use AI," they want screening and search to just be faster and more accurate.

Designing one system that genuinely works for both a five-person MSME team and a large corporate hiring department was a real scaling exercise, and the early adoption feedback was a reminder that even a well-designed system needs space for people to get comfortable before it becomes part of how they actually work.
